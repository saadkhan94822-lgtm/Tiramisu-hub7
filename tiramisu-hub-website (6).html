Our Story

A Motihari Dessert Destination

Tiramisu Hub started with one simple idea: Motihari deserved a place to get proper, fresh tiramisu — not a slice that's been sitting in a display case for days.

Every box we send out is layered, dusted, and packed the same day, near Janpul, in front of Smart Bazar. Whether you're celebrating something big or just craving something rich and creamy, we make it feel like an occasion.

No shortcuts, no artificial shelf-life tricks — just honest ingredients, made fresh, made local.
